What to Do If Your Vape Falls Into Water: Step-by-Step Guide

Accidentally dropped your vape kit in the sink, toilet, or a puddle, or perhaps, the washing machine? Don't panic. It occurs to a higher number than you would imagine, and it is not always that your equipment is dead. The first few minutes count a lot and after this, below is a step by step guide on how to present the best chances of survival to your vape.

Here's exactly what to do, step by step:

Step 1: Get It Out of the Water Immediately

Don't leave it sitting there. The longer the device stays submerged, the more water can enter the battery compartment, coil, and circuit board. Pull it out immediately, but don't shake it excessively. Shaking it forcefully can push water deeper into the device instead of helping it drain.

Step 2: Power It Off (Do Not Try to Vape It)

Don't press the fire button to see if it still works. That's the last thing you should do. When there is water anywhere around the battery, switching it on may short it, destroy the battery permanently, or in extreme cases, may make it overheat. When it is already off, then leave it off at the time.

Step 3: Take It Apart as Much as Possible

This is the most important step for pod kits and refillable pods. Separate every component you can:

  • Remove the pod or tank from the battery/mod
  • Take out the coil if accessible
  • Remove any removable batteries
  • Take off the mouthpiece or drip tip

With a vape that is disposed of, then you will not be able to open it up as they are sealed. You can continue with the next step however remember sealed devices will be more apt to being permanently damaged as the water cannot easily escape either.

Step 4: Dry Everything Thoroughly

  • Clean all the parts using a clean cloth or paper towel.
  • In case you have compressed air, blow the water through small holes, air-flow holes, and the charging port.
  • Skip the hairdryer. The plastic may be distorted by heat and the battery disordered.
  • Allow to dry at a place with warmth at least 24-48 hours. You need not have rice, it scarcely matters. Fresh air does better.

Step 5: Check the Charging Port and Contacts

Most long term problems are as a result of this. When it is fully dry, check the charging port and battery contacts to the presence of a white or chalky residue. Mineral deposition of the water. Use a dry cotton swab to clean it off, or moistened with isopropyl alcohol, with a little water.

Step 6: Reassemble and Test Carefully

After everything is completely dry, that is not only dry to touch, but you are certain that it has fully dried, reassemble it and trial it in a well ventilated area, where there is nothing combustible about it. Should it start and shoot like usual, then you have nothing to worry about. Unless it will accept the charge, there is a spark or a burning smell, or the battery is hot, then stop and never use it again.

Step 7: Know When to Replace vs. Repair

Sometimes a wet vape just won't come back, even if you do everything right.

Situation What to do
Coil or pod got wet, battery/mod is fine Replace just the pod or coil
Battery won't hold a charge, or device won't power on after drying Replace the battery or device
Disposable stopped working after water exposure Replace the whole unit - can't be repaired
Charging port corroded Clean gently; replace if charging still fails

Quick Recap

  1. Remove from water immediately
  2. Power off - don't test it wet
  3. Disassemble what you can
  4. Air dry for 24-48 hours (no heat)
  5. Check and clean charging port/contacts
  6. Reassemble and test carefully
  7. Replace parts as needed
